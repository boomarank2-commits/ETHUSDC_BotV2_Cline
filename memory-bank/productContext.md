# Product Context

The bot should become a realistic ETHUSDC Spot LONG-only trading system.

Main idea:
Learn recurring market situations from training data and test the frozen result on blindtest data.

The blindtest should later provide an expectation frame for Paper and Live, including monthly and period-based comparison.

Later, a Test Trade should execute exactly one complete trade after conscious configuration takeover and collect diagnostic comparison data against backtest expectations.

The bot should not be a fixed single-strategy bot.
The bot should not trade every market phase.
